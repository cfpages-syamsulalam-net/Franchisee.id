## Purpose

This file is the shared pre-writing evidence layer for the complete `franchisee.id` catalog. It consolidates current Indonesian franchise status, registration, licensing, disclosure, contract, IP, competition, commercial, workforce, consumer, data, dispute, and operating evidence once, then maps it to every parent topic.

It is not a franchise recommendation, investment forecast, prospectus, contract review, legal/tax/accounting/credit advice, compliance certification, or proof that a listed brand is genuine or profitable. Writers must open and cite the original source, distinguish law from practical guidance, date every volatile fact, and resolve the stated gates.

## How writers must use this file

1. Start with the relevant topic row and open every mapped original source.
2. Use official regulation pages to establish current status and public scope; use the current full text and qualified Indonesian advisers for clause-level conclusions.
3. Separate a franchise offer, the legal entity offering it, registered IP, the operating system, one outlet, and the whole network.
4. Separate historical results, current verified facts, assumptions, scenarios, forecasts, testimonials, and guarantees.
5. Treat fees, tax, financing, licences, wages, prices, unit economics, and market data as dated and entity/site/sector-specific.
6. Treat directory data and submitted documents as evidence candidates, not independent verification.
7. Preserve stop conditions for identity mismatch, unofficial payment, missing documents, unverifiable claims, inadequate liquidity, unsafe premises, and pressure to sign before review.
8. Recheck volatile sources during outlining or six months after the date above, whichever comes first.

## Evidence scale

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Current Indonesian law, official regulation record, or official licensing source | Establish status, jurisdiction, public scope, and high-level duties |
| B | Official regulator, statistics, IP, competition, or tax guidance | Explain official processes and bounded evidence with date and scope |
| C | Recognized international official framework used only as comparative context | Explain a method or cross-border concept; never convert it into Indonesian law |
| Gate | Offer-, entity-, site-, sector-, transaction-, or professional-specific evidence remains unresolved | Do not publish a definitive legal, financial, compliance, performance, or recommendation claim |

### KR-02 — Current Indonesian franchise framework

- **Sources:** [PP No. 35 Tahun 2024 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/297489/pp-no-35-tahun-), [official PP 35/2024 PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/357030/PP%20Nomor%2035%20Tahun%202024.pdf).
- **Grade:** A.
- **Purpose:** Establish the current central franchise framework and retire advice based on the revoked PP 42/2007.
- **Summary:** PP 35/2024 is in force from 2 September 2024, revoked PP 42/2007, and publicly scopes franchise criteria, prospectus, agreement, rights/duties, STPW, logo, domestic products, reporting, supervision, prohibitions, and sanctions.
- **Grounded facts:** “Franchise” is a regulated relationship, not a marketing synonym for any licence, dealership, agency, partnership, or business package. Both role and offer documents matter.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `FRC-01`, `FRC-03`, `FRC-05`, `FRC-06`, `FRC-08`, `FRC-14`, `FRC-15`, `FRC-16`; date the framework and link the full text.
- **Limits/recheck:** A general summary cannot determine whether one arrangement legally qualifies, complies, or creates a particular remedy.

- **Grounded facts:** The applicant role, domestic/foreign origin, upstream franchisor status, required form/agreement, competent authority, and current OSS workflow must be identified before describing a path.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `FRC-03`, `FRC-05`, `FRC-06`, `FRC-08`, `FRC-15`; create role-specific checklists rather than one universal “register franchise” article.
- **Limits/recheck:** Resolve the interaction among PP 35/2024, Permendag 71/2019, Permendag 25/2025, later rules, OSS configuration, and local practice with current counsel/authority evidence.

### KR-04 — Risk-based business licensing and OSS

- **Sources:** [PP No. 28 Tahun 2025 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/319773/pp-no-28-tahun-), [Permeninves/BKPM No. 5 Tahun 2025 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/332573), [official OSS STPW guide example](https://oss.go.id/id/panduan/635970086345c7d71a8144bd).
- **Grade:** A/B.
- **Purpose:** Separate franchise registration from the licences needed for the actual outlet, entity, location, and sector.
- **Summary:** PP 28/2025 replaced PP 5/2021 and governs risk-based business licensing, supporting licences, OSS services, supervision, and sanctions. The exact licence set depends on classified activity and risk.
- **Grounded facts:** An STPW does not by itself prove that premises, food, health, education, construction, transport, environmental, labour, signage, or other sector/location requirements are satisfied.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `FRC-05`, `FRC-07`, `FRC-08`, `FRC-09`, `FRC-12`; build a project-specific licence register.
- **Limits/recheck:** Confirm current KBLI, risk level, applicant/entity, location, sector standards, transition rules, and OSS display at the transaction date.

### KR-06 — Agreement structure, responsibility, and lifecycle events

### KR-09 — Market and network data are bounded samples

- **Sources:** [BPS analysis of modern-system businesses including franchises](https://www.bps.go.id/id/publication/2017/12/26/99bbeef5ff921a70871364a7/analisis-hasil-listing-sensus-ekonomi-2016----usaha-dengan-sistem-modern--peranan--sebaran--dan-karakteristik.html), [BPS Statistics of Business Characteristics 2021](https://www.bps.go.id/id/publication/2021/12/17/4e90dd21d3bf177e497a92c7/statistikkarakteristik-usaha-2021.html).
