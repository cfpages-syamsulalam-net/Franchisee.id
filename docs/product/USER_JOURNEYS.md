Reviewed 24 September 2026 against the repository at `1646f3a`. This is a guide to the behavior implemented in code, not a claim that every signed-in or payment step has been tested in production. Start with [what to do next](NEXT_STEPS.md); the underlying product plans are linked below.

A member can add the other public role through `/profil` (`add_public_role`); role badges in the browser are hints, while D1 authorization is the server boundary. An unclaimed brand's listed city is a recorded location, not a promise that the brand is accepting partners there. A franchisee buyer and a franchise owner are distinct personas even when the same account has both roles.

## Follow the handoffs

1. **Discover → decide:** the visitor filters the directory, opens a listing, compares costs and information, and chooses save, contact or claim. Check whether the brand and modal claims are supported by the listing data; details and contact data on an unclaimed profile are not owner-verified (`src/lib/franchise-static.ts`, `src/lib/franchise-buyer-tools.ts`).
2. **Identity → completion:** Clerk creates/authenticates the account; `/auth-sync` maps it to D1 roles; `/daftar` completes role-specific information. `/profil` shows the useful next action, existing account information and role-specific work. Never interpret signing up alone as a completed buyer profile or a published brand (`js/auth-clerk.js`, `functions/auth-sync.js`, `functions/form-submit.js`, `functions/profile-data.js`).
3. **Interest → lead → response:** a signed-in buyer saves or sends an inquiry; the owned franchisor listing's lead inbox and dashboard Leads expose follow-up work. Confirm that the inquiry is visible to the intended owner, has actionable contact details and has a recoverable duplicate/denied state (`functions/profile-data.js`, `js/profile-leads.js`, `functions/_dashboard-queries.js`).
4. **Brand update → public page:** a form submission, permitted owner edit or admin approval writes D1 and enqueues `site_rebuild_requests`. A queue poller/Deploy Hook rebuilds public Astro pages. An API success does **not** prove that the public page changed (`functions/_site-publish-queue.js`, `.github/workflows/d1-static-publish.yaml`, `scripts/build-d1-franchise-pages.ts`).
5. **Premium order → decision → renewal:** an owner creates a manual bank-transfer order from `/profil`, uploads/records payment confirmation, admin reviews in `/dashboard`, subscription/placement updates and public rebuild follow; scheduled reminders and expiry handling are separate. Avoid calling a payment complete until the order, subscription and public state agree (`functions/_profile-premium.js`, `functions/_dashboard-actions.js`, [Premium plan](../architecture/PREMIUM_MONETIZATION_PLAN.md)).
6. **Claim → review → ownership:** the intended product rule is pending claim, evidence review, then ownership/publication on approval ([claim matrix](../forms/CLAIM_TRANSITION_MATRIX.md)). The current submit implementation differs; do not use this path as proof of brand ownership until priority 1 is resolved.

## Business risks and abuse cases by journey

A filled form is an applicant's account of a business, not evidence that the applicant controls that business. Each persona journey needs a safe outcome when a participant acts dishonestly or makes a mistake.

| Journey | Legitimate business outcome | Abuse or failure to consider | Protection and unresolved proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor finds a brand | Compare opportunities and contact a trustworthy counterparty. | An unclaimed profile or self-reported financial figures may be mistaken for verified brand information; a scammer can send prospects to a false payment/contact destination. | Distinguish unclaimed/owner-confirmed and sourced/unsourced data in public copy; do not equate a listing with vetting. Independently verify high-stakes contact and investment claims before adding trust labels. |
| Buyer saves or inquires | Follow up with the selected brand without losing the inquiry. | A stranger reads another buyer's leads, or a fabricated listing harvests buyer contact details. | Check account-scoped reads/writes; display who receives the inquiry and preserve consent. Test with two different buyer accounts and one deliberately unclaimed listing. |
| New franchisor submits | Bring a genuinely new brand to prospective buyers. | A stranger registers another company's name as a *new* listing, or invents ROI/contacts to solicit payment. | An authenticated account alone is not proof of brand ownership; reject an exact existing brand name and review consequential trust signals. **Open product risk:** new-brand submissions still publish as `free` without independent ownership review. |
| Representative claims existing brand | Correct an existing listing and access their leads only after ownership is established. | A third party fills every required field, claims someone else's brand, replaces contact details and diverts prospects or money. | Keep submitted details private in `pending` claim; public listing, owner and leads remain unchanged. Show claimant identity, NIB/HAKI *as unverified assertions*. Admin independently verifies authority through an established brand channel and records source/outcome before approval. Reject/hold when evidence conflicts; no auto-approval from NIB, HAKI, a matching name or a complete form. |
| Staff handles outreach | Contact brands with a record of follow-up. | Mistaken/surreptitious contact export or staff approving their own ownership claim. | Account-level Google Contacts authorization, audit events, and role separation; staff can prepare, admin decides ownership. |
| Admin decides claim or payment | Resolve a real claim/payment with a traceable decision. | A rushed reviewer approves a convincing fake document, stale claim races an existing owner, or unsupported payment is marked paid. | Review applicant details against an independent source; record verification notes; database guard refuses approval once listing has an owner. Keep payment evidence and role checks separate. Manual verification can still be deceived, so keep audit history and a reversal/escalation route. |

**Controlled acceptance scenario:** use a disposable unclaimed brand and two separate applicants. One can submit a complete but false claim; neither applicant can edit the public listing or see its owner leads while pending. Admin must record independent verification before approving one; a later or conflicting approval cannot replace that owner. Rejection never changes the listing. Never test this with a real brand or real buyer payment.

### Existing-brand choice before form completion
When a franchisor types a brand name already in the directory, the registration form shows the existing listing early. An unclaimed entry without a pending claim offers a direct claim of that exact listing; a pending claim is marked under review; a managed entry links to its public page. A business PIC and phone appear in this notice only after admin-approved ownership and public publication. An exact duplicate cannot silently create a competing new listing. These controls do not prove ownership of a genuinely new or deliberately misspelled brand; the new-brand review risk remains in [next steps](NEXT_STEPS.md).
